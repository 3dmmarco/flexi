introduction

having its origin rooted in a design course at TU Wien, faculty of architecture, this project investigated topics like bio-based materials, digital production processes and experimental formfinding. through iterative physical model studies, interweaving properties of soft wool-based fabrics with bio-plastic based material matrixes serving as hardening agents. learning from these experiments a structural logic was formed and applied to construct FLEXI with the help of digital production methods through panelization and adaptie molds.
