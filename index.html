introduction

having its origin rooted in a design course at TU Wien, faculty of architecture, this project investigated topics like bio-based materials, digital production processes and experimental formfinding. through iterative physical model studies, interweaving properties of soft wool-based fabrics with bio-plastic based material matrixes serving as hardening agents. learning from these experiments a structural logic was formed and applied to construct FLEXI with the help of digital production methods through panelization and adaptive molds.

a spine leans into the air transforming into a wing.

It feels like the peak of a movement,

a muscle contracting,

a weave like muscle fibers drawn close together,

Closer to the ground the forms surrender to gravity,

soft as a limb at rest.

not enclosing,
