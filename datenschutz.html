General

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This privacy policy explains what data is processed when you visit this website and on what legal basis, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG).

Hosting & Server Logs

When you visit this website, your browser automatically transmits data to the web server, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and date and time of access. This data is temporarily stored in server log files for technical and security purposes and deleted after a short period. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure operation of the website).

Google Fonts

This website uses Google Fonts, a typeface service provided by Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. When you load a page, your browser requests the fonts directly from Google's servers, which causes your IP address to be transmitted to Google. This constitutes a transfer of personal data to a third country (USA).

The legal basis for this processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in the consistent and visually correct presentation of this website. We have assessed that this interest is not overridden by your rights and interests given the limited nature of the data transmitted.

For more information on how Google handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies and does not employ any tracking, analytics, or advertising tools.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability regarding your personal data. You also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise these rights, please contact us at flexi@sexy.at.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde): dsb.gv.at.
